Our Story

A family business since 1984

Audi Contractors began in the DC Metro area in 1984, with Victor, a builder with decades of residential and commercial experience. His son Jamil opened the family’s first design center in 2011 and later merged the design and contracting businesses into KBF by Audi Contractors, so design and construction now sit under one roof.
